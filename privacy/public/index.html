RALLY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Rally is a watch-only Apple Watch app for keeping score during matches and optionally recording a volleyball workout. This policy explains what data Rally uses and how it is handled.

Data Rally uses

  • Scores and team names. These stay on your Apple Watch. Rally stores them in an on-device App Group so the scoreboard and Smart Stack widget can show the same score.
  • Health and fitness data. If you start a volleyball session, Rally uses Apple HealthKit to read heart rate and active energy during the workout, and to save the finished workout (including the final score as workout metadata) to Apple Health.

Data Rally does not collect

Rally does not create an account, does not send your score or Health data to our servers, and does not sell or share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Where data is stored

Scoreboard data remains on your watch. HealthKit data is managed by Apple Health on your devices according to your Apple ID and Health settings. You can revoke Health access for Rally in the Health app or watch Settings at any time.

Permissions

Rally asks for Health permission only when you start a volleyball workout. You can decline and keep using the scoreboard without recording a workout.

Children

Rally is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of Rally after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Rally privacy practices: privacy@itwasmattgregg.com
