Display the score on iPad

Score on Apple Watch. Use the iPhone companion when you want a bigger board — including a court-facing iPad — with no internet on the court.

Why use the iOS companion?

Rally on Apple Watch works on its own. You can keep two scores, bump them up or down, and play without installing anything on iPhone or iPad. There is no sport picker and no win logic — just two numbers that can climb as high as you need.

Install the iOS companion when you want the score somewhere people can see it from across the court:

  • Phone board — your paired iPhone shows the live score while you keep scoring on the Watch.
  • iPad display — put a larger board on the sideline by joining the phone’s Host session from the iPad app (QR or short code).
  • Offline on court — linking stays local. You do not need venue Wi‑Fi or a cloud account for the scoreboard path.

The companion is for linking displays. Scoring stays on the Watch.

How to show the score on an iPad

Watch scores → iPhone Host (relay + board) → iPad Join (court display)

  1. Score on your Watch. Start a Rally session as usual. The Watch remains the place you tap points.
  2. Open Rally on your iPhone and Host. The phone shows the live board and a QR code / short join code for nearby displays. Keep Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi on so devices can find each other; you still do not need internet.
  3. On the iPad, open Rally and Join. Scan the phone’s QR code, or enter the code / pick the nearby session. The iPad becomes a full-screen scoreboard — display only.
  4. Leave the phone on the floor if you need to. It may lock while Hosting; the session should keep feeding the iPad. Keep the iPad awake so the court board stays visible.

If the iPad disconnects, Join again with the same QR or code. Extra iPads can Join the same Host if you want more than one board.

What Rally does not do

  • No accounts or cloud score sync for the court display path.
  • No sport rules, games, sets, or automatic winners.
  • No scoring controls on the phone or iPad in this path — Watch only.
